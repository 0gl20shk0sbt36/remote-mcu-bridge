# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be free of harassment for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be civil and assume good faith; disagree with the technical content, not the person.
- Keep review comments specific, actionable and about the code.
- Respect the security boundary of the project: never ask others to share
  device credentials, private firmware images or logs containing them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's
private information, or deliberately introducing malicious code.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any project channel.
Maintainers may edit, lock or remove contributions that violate it, and may ban
repeat offenders. Report problems privately to the maintainer listed in
`SECURITY.md`; reports are handled confidentially.
